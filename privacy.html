Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Gayson Loser Agentic Bot application ("the App") handles information in connection with its TikTok integration.

1. Information We Collect

The App operates server-to-server and does not collect, store, or process personal information about end users. It holds only its own application credentials (Client key and secret) and publishes content to its own linked TikTok account.

2. How We Use Information

Application credentials are used solely to authenticate API requests for publishing the App's own original content. No user data, viewer data, or audience analytics are collected or processed.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties. API requests are made directly to TikTok's servers for the sole purpose of publishing content.

4. Data Security

Credentials are stored securely on the operator's systems and are never exposed to end users or third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with TikTok via the official Content Posting API. TikTok's own data practices are governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy, which is separate from this Policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the account operator via the TikTok profile associated with the App.
